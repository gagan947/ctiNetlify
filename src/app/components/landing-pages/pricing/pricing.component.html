How pricing works

At Creative AI, we think you should only pay for what you need. That’s why you won’t find price lists on our site. Instead, we have a fully transparent, feature-based pricing model no hidden fees, no nasty surprises.

Step

Choose Base

Vanya, Your AI Product Manager.

Share your app or website idea by typing, chatting, recording, or uploading or pick a ready-made template that matches your vision. Vanya instantly turns it into the perfect project base so you can build exactly what you imagine.

Make It Mine

Vanya, Your Personalization Hub.

In this step, you can easily upload your logo, choose your app name, and add your website URL. Instantly see a live preview of how your app or website will look on both desktop and mobile. Make it truly yours before moving to the next stage of your project journey!

Refine Idea

Vanya, Your Smart Feature Planner.

In this step, Vanya shows you all the features included in your app or website, along with the cost and estimated time to develop each one. You can customize the features, add or remove what you need, and see real-time updates on pricing and timelines. Refine your idea until it fits your vision and budget perfectly!

Plan Delivery

Vanya, Your Project Roadmap Guide.

Vanya helps you confirm all the important details choose your target platforms like Android, iOS, web, or mobile site. Pick your development speed: standard, fast, or super-fast, depending on your needs. Set project phases, delivery milestones, and get a clickable prototype to see exactly how your app or website will work before development begins.

Review Build Card

Vanya, Your Project Snapshot.

In this step, Vanya shows you a clear build summary with your chosen platforms Android, iOS, web, or mobile site along with the total project cost, number of features, and estimated delivery date. Get a final overview before you move ahead with full confidence.

Billing Details

Vanya, Your Secure Checkout.

Here, you’ll enter your billing details and confirm your contact information. Vanya keeps your data safe and makes sure your payment and project information are accurate before you start building.

Payment Plan

Vanya, Your Flexible Payment Partner.

No need to pay everything upfront choose what works for you. Click to pick a weekly or monthly payment option, and Vanya shows you clear plans to match your budget and timeline.
