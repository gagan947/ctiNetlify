Cancellation & Refund Policy

Thank you for choosing our services. We understand that sometimes plans change. This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines how refunds are handled for transactions processed through Razorpay International.

1. Cancellation Policy

Users may request to cancel their orders or subscriptions at any time before the service/product delivery. Please note:

  • Cancellation requests should be sent to our support email: info@creativethoughtsinfo.com.
  • Cancellation requests received after the service/product delivery may not be eligible for a refund.
  • For subscription services, cancellation will prevent further billing but does not retroactively refund the previous charges.

2. Refund Policy

We process refunds according to the following conditions:

  • Refunds are initiated within 7-10 business days from approval of the cancellation request.
  • The refund will be credited to the original payment method used during the purchase.
  • Bank processing times may vary depending on your bank and location.
  • For any disputes regarding international transactions, we follow Razorpay’s international transaction guidelines.
Important: All refunds are subject to verification and approval by our support team. Any fraudulent or duplicate requests will be declined.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions about cancellations or refunds, please reach out to our support team at info@creativethoughtsinfo.com.

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Cancellation & Refund Policy.
